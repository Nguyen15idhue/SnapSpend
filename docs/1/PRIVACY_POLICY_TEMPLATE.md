# SnapSpend Privacy Policy — template

> Đây là template kỹ thuật. Không publish nguyên văn trước khi điền pháp nhân, domain, email, thời hạn lưu trữ và nhà cung cấp thật.

## 1. Data collected

SnapSpend may process account information, user-selected photographs, expense amounts, expense dates, categories, notes and aggregated statistics.

## 2. Why data is processed

Data is processed to provide expense tracking, photo storage, categorization, statistics and optional AI analysis.

## 3. AI processing

When AI is enabled, selected data may be sent to the configured AI provider. The production privacy notice must identify the provider, purposes, retention behavior and applicable user controls.

## 4. Sharing

Expenses are private by default. Data is shared with another user only when the owner explicitly uses the sharing function.

## 5. Deletion

Users may delete their account. Account deletion removes the user account and user-owned relational data according to the application's deletion policy.
